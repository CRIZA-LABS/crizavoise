- Publish a modified version, fork or rebranded build under the name CrizaVoise or with the CrizaVoise or Criza Labs logos.

## Before you publish a fork
1. Give it its own name and icon. Replace the files in `assets/brand/` and the name in `lib/common/app_config.dart`.
2. Keep `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
3. Show the credit line **"Based on CrizaVoise by Criza Labs (https://criza.dev)"** in your README and your app's About screen.
